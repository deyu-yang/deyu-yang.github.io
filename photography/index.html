回忆 · Memory

毕业考试、答辩结束后的第二天，在巴拉顿湖边的一个圆形茅草顶餐厅吃午饭，一向不善饮酒的我，主动点了一大杯扎啤。

The day after my final exams and thesis defense, I had lunch at this round, thatched-roof restaurant by Lake Balaton. Never much of a drinker, I ordered a large draft beer all on my own.

回忆 · Memory

初到匈牙利两年后第一次准备回国的前一晚，我在傍晚沿着多瑙河走到国会大厦边，那一刻我心中充满着对回到阔别两年的家的憧憬，同时竟也感受到了那么一丝要离开的不舍，到底是对什么的不舍呢？后来我意识到，我的整个19、20岁都留在了那里，我的妈妈和妹妹，没有见到过20岁的我。

The evening before my first trip home, two years after I arrived in Hungary, I walked along the Danube to the Parliament. I was full of longing for the home I had not seen in two years, yet, to my surprise, I also felt a faint reluctance to leave. Reluctance for what? Later I realized that my whole nineteenth and twentieth years had stayed there. My mother and my sister never saw me at twenty.

回忆 · Memory

那天我独自在佩奇走了很远很远的路，我想要走到远处山坡顶上的电视塔那里去，但我没有走到，我还要赶上晚上返回布达佩斯的火车，我只好不甘地往回走着，这是我在半路走出树木遮蔽的道路向远处眺望所看到的，那一刻我想着，在未来我还会有机会再次来到这里，走到那个电视塔下吗？

That day I walked a long, long way alone in Pécs, hoping to reach the TV tower on the distant hilltop. I never made it. I had an evening train back to Budapest to catch, so I turned back, reluctantly. Halfway down, the road came out from under the trees, and this is what I saw when I looked into the distance. In that moment I wondered: will I ever come back here and walk all the way to that TV tower?
